# 📊 Vrinda Store Annual Report 2022  

This project is an **Excel Dashboard** built to analyze and visualize sales performance for **Vrinda Store** in 2022. The goal of this report is to help the business understand its **sales trends, customer demographics, order channels, and regional performance** in order to make data-driven decisions.  

---

## 🚀 Project Overview  
- Created an **interactive Excel Dashboard** with slicers (Month, Platform, Category).  
- Visualized **sales vs orders trend** to identify seasonality.  
- Compared **sales by gender, age group, and region**.  
- Analyzed **top-performing states and sales channels**.  
- Measured **order status breakdown** (delivered, refunded, returned, cancelled).  

---

## 📈 Key Insights  
1. **Orders vs Sales**:  
   - Sales peaked around mid-year (May–Aug).  
   - Decline observed in the last quarter, requiring seasonal planning.  

2. **Gender Contribution**:  
   - Women contributed **64%** of total sales vs **36%** from men.  

3. **Order Status**:  
   - **92% orders delivered successfully**, while only 8% faced issues (returned, refunded, cancelled).  

4. **Top States by Sales**:  
   - Maharashtra (2.98M)  
   - Karnataka (2.65M)  
   - Uttar Pradesh (2.10M)  
   - Telangana (1.71M)  
   - Tamil Nadu (1.68M)  

5. **Sales Channels**:  
   - Amazon (35%) was the leading sales channel.  
   - Flipkart (22%) and Myntra (23%) also had strong performance.  

6. **Demographics**:  
   - Majority buyers were **adult women** (~39% of total sales).  
   - Teenagers also contributed significantly.
